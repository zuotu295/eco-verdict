How a verdict is reached

I

The filing

A claim and its sources are written to the docket exactly as filed. Nothing is edited afterwards; a corrected claim is a new filing.

II

The fetching

Each validator independently loads the filed sources live from the open web. Page text is read, never stored, and never compared.

III

The consensus

Validators must agree exactly on the verdict and the reason category. Only then is the verdict sealed on-chain; summary wording may differ.

IV

The weight of proof

Independent certification registries outweigh company self-statements. A 100% claim backed only by a future goal is greenwash, not a close call.

Honest limits: the court judges the filed sources only. It does not audit internal company records, test products in a lab, or prove that a source document itself is genuine.
